---
id: G-005
title: Miter gate
aliases:
  - mitre gate
created: "2023-01-12"
---

A gate made of two leaves that meet at an angle pointing upstream, so the water's own pressure forces them shut and seals the chamber. Because it holds head from only one direction, the chamber must be brought level with the gate's side before the gate can open. See decision [[D-002]].
