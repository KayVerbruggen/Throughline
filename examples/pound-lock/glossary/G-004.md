title: Sluice
aliases:
  - paddle
  - valve
created: "2023-01-12"
---

The valve that lets water flow between a reach and the **chamber** to change the chamber's level. The upstream sluice fills the chamber from the upper reach; the downstream sluice drains it to the lower reach. A sluice may only open while both gates are closed.
