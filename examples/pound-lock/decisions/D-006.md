In how components are serviced and tested, facing crews cannot exercise a single gate or sluice while the passage interlock forbids unsafe states, we decided to add a maintenance mode that lets each actuator be driven independently once the lock is taken out of service and not to require the interlock to be physically disconnected for servicing to achieve any component can be tested in isolation without defeating the safety wiring permanently, accepting that while in maintenance mode safety depends on crew procedure, not the interlock.
