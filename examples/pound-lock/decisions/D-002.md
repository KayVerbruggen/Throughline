In the choice of gate type for each end of the chamber, facing the gates must seal against the head of water and be cheap to build and maintain on a small canal, we decided to fit miter gates that the water pressure itself forces shut and not to use sector gates or vertical-lift gates to achieve a self-sealing gate with simple, low-cost machinery, accepting that a miter gate only holds head from one direction, so the chamber must always be filled or drained to the sill before opening.
