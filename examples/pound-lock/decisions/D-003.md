---
id: D-003
title: Emergency stop freezes and latches, never auto-drives
status: accepted
trace:
  - UC-004
context: behaviour of the emergency stop
concern: automatically driving gates to a safe position during a fault could move a vessel or worsen the situation
decision: to halt every actuator in place and latch the stop until an operator releases it
alternatives: to auto-close the gates and equalise the chamber on a stop
criterion: predictable behaviour with no surprise motion after a stop is pressed
downside: the operator must recover the lock by hand before normal service resumes
created: "2023-01-20"
---

In the behaviour of the emergency stop, facing automatically driving gates to a safe position during a fault could move a vessel or worsen the situation, we decided to halt every actuator in place and latch the stop until an operator releases it and not to auto-close the gates and equalise the chamber on a stop to achieve predictable behaviour with no surprise motion after a stop is pressed, accepting that the operator must recover the lock by hand before normal service resumes.
