In the safety architecture of the lock, facing independent local interlocks on each gate could disagree and leave both ends of the chamber open at once, we decided to route every gate, sluice and signal through a single interlock controller that owns the safety sequence and not to give each gate its own local interlock wired to its neighbour to achieve one place where the never-both-gates-open invariant is enforced and can be reviewed, accepting that the controller becomes a single point whose failure stops all passage.
