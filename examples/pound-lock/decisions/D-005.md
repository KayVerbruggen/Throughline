In the first control concept for the lock, facing each gate and sluice needs a way to be driven, we decided to give every gate and sluice its own local panel at the gate and not to centralise control on the quay to achieve short, simple wiring runs from each panel to its actuator, accepting that the operator has to walk between panels and no single place enforces the interlock.
