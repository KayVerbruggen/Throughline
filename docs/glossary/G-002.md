title: Structure connection
aliases:
  - connection
  - edge
created: "2026-07-14"
---

An undirected link between two components. Connections are never authored or stored: they are derived from flow adjacency — two components are connected exactly when their activities run back-to-back in some flow. This derivation is the tool's core consistency rule (see [[D-001]]).
