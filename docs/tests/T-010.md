Asserts that the interpreter descends into a called flow rather than stepping over
it: the callee's effects land in the caller's valuation, a guard inside the callee
sees state the caller wrote, and returning resumes at the call step so the caller's
later branches see the callee's writes. Also covers nested calls unwinding in order,
a recursive call terminating at the depth limit instead of hanging, and an invoke
with no resolvable target staying an opaque pass-through.
