In the traceability spine, facing bidirectional links double the bookkeeping and can end up disagreeing with each other, we decided to store trace links forward-only (child to parent) and compute every reverse lookup at render time and not to also store reverse links, or model a free-form graph of typed links to achieve one authored direction that can never contradict its own inverse, accepting that a component appearing in no flow stays invisible to the spine — there is no requirement-to-component allocation link.
